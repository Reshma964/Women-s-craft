Terms & Conditions

Please Read Carefully

1. What is WomenCraft?

WomenCraft is an online e-commerce platform dedicated to showcasing and selling handmade products created by talented women artisans. Our mission is to empower women through entrepreneurship by giving them a digital space to promote, sell, and grow their unique crafts and cultural products.

2. What kind of products can I find?

You can find a wide variety of handcrafted items, including home décor, jewelry, traditional textiles, handwoven baskets, eco-friendly gifts, and personalized artisan goods — all made with care and creativity by women across India.

3. Who are the sellers?

Our sellers are skilled women artisans, self-help group members, and small business owners from rural and urban India. We partner directly with them to ensure fair pricing and support sustainable income for their families.

4. How fast do you deliver?

We deliver to you in the next hour, or at any other one-hour time-slot today and in the next 6 days.

5. How much does delivery cost?

Delivery charges depend on the product size and location. We offer free delivery on all orders above ₹999. Shipping costs will be displayed at checkout before payment.

6. Are the products sustainable and eco-friendly?

Yes! Most of our products are made using traditional, natural, or upcycled materials. Supporting these artisans means promoting a more sustainable and ethical way of shopping.

7. Can I customize or personalize items?

Absolutely! Many of our products can be personalized. You can select the “Customize” option on the product page or mention your preferences in the note section at checkout.

8. How are artisans supported through this platform?

We provide artisans with training, access to digital tools, marketing support, and fair trade opportunities. A large portion of the sales goes directly to the artisans to help them become financially independent.

9. What if an item is out of stock?

If a product is out of stock, you can sign up for a restock alert on the product page. Alternatively, we can suggest similar handcrafted alternatives from the same artisan or region.

10. Can I return or exchange items?

Yes, we accept returns and exchanges for damaged or incorrect products within 7 days of delivery. Since most items are handmade, slight variations are part of their charm and are not considered defects.

11. How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you’ll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also check the “My Orders” section on your account dashboard for real-time status.

12. What payment methods are available?

We accept all major payment methods: credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and popular wallets like Paytm and PhonePe. Cash on Delivery (COD) is also available for select locations.

13. How do I contact customer support?

You can reach out to our support team anytime via the “Contact Us” page, email at support@womencraft.in, or use the live chat on the website. We’re happy to help!

14. Can I become a seller on WomenCraft?

Yes! If you are a woman artisan or a women-led craft business, apply through the “Join as a Seller” page. Our team will review and get in touch with onboarding details.

15. Do you support bulk or corporate gifting orders?

Yes, we offer bulk purchases and customized corporate gifting options with handcrafted packaging. Visit our “Bulk Orders” section or contact us for more information.

16. Are there any loyalty or reward programs?

We’re working on launching a WomenCraft Rewards Program soon, where you can earn points on purchases, referrals, and more. Stay tuned!
