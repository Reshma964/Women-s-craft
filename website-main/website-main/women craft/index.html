Why women craft

Straight from the makers

Our maker-to-you concept focuses on delivering handcrafted items made by women artisans directly to you, ensuring every piece comes straight from the creator’s hands with authenticity, care, and meaning..

Know Your makers

We want you to know exactly who is making your products by featuring each artisan’s profile on every item and a dedicated maker’s page. You’re welcome to explore their stories, see their creative process, and feel the passion they put into every handmade piece..

Supporting and Uplifting Women Artisans’ Livelihoods

Slowly but surely, by removing middlemen and simplifying the supply chain, we aim to improve the livelihoods of women artisans by ensuring they receive the fair earnings they deserve for their craftsmanship and dedication..
